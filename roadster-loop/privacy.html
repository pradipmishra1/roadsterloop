What the game stores

Roadster Loop saves your best score in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit or sell personal information through the game itself.

Third-party libraries

The game loads three.js from the cdnjs content delivery network, which may log standard request data such as your IP address.

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
